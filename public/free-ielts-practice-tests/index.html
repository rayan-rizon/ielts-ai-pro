How to use IELTS Ai Pro effectively

Start with one timed practice activity and review the result before moving to another task. Reading and Listening attempts show answer-level performance. Writing and Speaking activities can add estimated AI feedback when your plan includes an available evaluation. Save each attempt so the analytics dashboard can show patterns across skills instead of treating one result as your final level.

Use the feedback to choose one specific change for the next session: improve timing, revise an essay paragraph, expand a speaking answer, review unfamiliar vocabulary, or repeat a weak question type. Focused repetition makes the practice history more useful and helps you avoid random study.

Understand practice estimates

AI-assisted bands and comments are preparation guidance, not official IELTS results. They are designed to organize feedback around relevant criteria and help you identify possible weaknesses. Always use official IELTS sources for current test rules, score requirements, and booking information. Official scores are issued only by authorized test providers after the real examination.
