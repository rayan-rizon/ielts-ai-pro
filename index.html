IELTS Ai Pro FAQ

What can I practice for free?
The Free plan includes IELTS Reading and Listening practice, Cambridge test sets available to free learners, one AI Writing evaluation per month, vocabulary tools, and basic progress analytics. Current limits are always shown on the pricing page.
How does the IELTS Writing feedback work?
After a practice task, IELTS Ai Pro returns an estimated practice band with feedback across Task Achievement or Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. The report also highlights specific corrections and next steps.
What does the Speaking report assess?
Speaking reports organize practice feedback around Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, and Pronunciation. When acoustic pronunciation evidence is unavailable, the product identifies transcript-based estimates instead of presenting them as measured pronunciation scores.
Are AI band scores official IELTS results?
No. AI band scores are practice estimates designed to help you find patterns and choose what to improve next. Official IELTS results are issued only by authorized IELTS test providers and trained examiners.
How is the AI learning path personalized?
For eligible plans, the learning path uses completed practice, skill performance, target band, and study schedule to recommend focused activities. You can review progress in the analytics dashboard and continue directly into the suggested module.
Does IELTS Ai Pro cover all four IELTS skills?
Yes. The app includes Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking practice, plus Cambridge test sets, vocabulary review, study resources, progress analytics, and an adaptive learning path for eligible plans.
